# In This World: Roblox Story Playbook

Copied from the playbook doc on Nov 18, 2026 (original: https://claude.ai/artifact/J5QtBa5EvWnEa65bJPV2oP, tied to Kalid's first Claude account).

## Bottom line

The winning formula is a narrated 1 to 2.5 minute fable: a strange rule, a kid who is the exception, and a twist that flips what the rule means. Of 87 story Shorts at least a week old, 49 passed 1M views (median 1.4M).

- **Premise is the biggest lever.** "Everyone can see your hidden stat" premises (lies, luck, IQ, word count) have the highest median, 4.55M. "X is deadly / banned" is the most common, with 19 of 28 over 1M.
- **Longer than you'd expect.** The 10 hits opened (2.3M to 35M views) run 59 to 166 seconds, narrated fast at about 4 words per second (roughly 250 words a minute), over Roblox avatars acting it out.
- **Same skeleton every time.** Rule, then 3 to 5 quick gags showing how the world works, then an outsider kid, a foil, a crisis, a twist, and an emotional payoff.
- **Still rising, but flooded.** New uploads hit 2.3M in 2 days, while dozens of vague "in this town roblox story" uploads sit at 1 to 50 views. Story quality is the difference.
- **A few channels dominate.** ItsGuestCap (35M, 29M, 7.1M) and Ryex (14M, 14M, 13M) own the top. Expect a new channel to need a run of uploads before any single video takes off.

## The story structure (timings for a 90 s video)

1. **The rule (0 to 3 s).** First line is always "In this world/town, [rule]." It doubles as the hook, so it must make sense with no context.
2. **World gags (3 to 15 s).** Three to five fast, funny, visual examples of life under the rule. This is where the replay value lives.
3. **The exception (15 to 25 s).** The narrator kid breaks the pattern.
4. **The foil (25 to 40 s).** Someone the rule judges wrongly.
5. **The crisis (40 to 60 s).** A public moment forces the rule into action.
6. **The twist (60 to 75 s).** A hidden truth flips what the rule means.
7. **The payoff (75 to 90 s).** Emotional release or callback gag, then a one-line moral or button. Several end with "Like and subscribe" worked into the last joke.

Narrator voice is plain short sentences. The emotion is family and belonging rather than horror. Weak videos used the same opening but had thin world gags and a twist with no family stakes.

## Script 2 premise: The footprints

Rule: in this town, your footprints glow the color of how you really feel. Gags: liars walk on tiptoe, couples check each other's prints on date night, the mayor rides a scooter everywhere. Exception: the new kid whose prints are always gold, which nobody has ever seen. Twist: gold isn't happiness; it's what your prints look like when you're pretending for someone else's sake. Payoff: the kid's mom sees the gold trail leading to the hospital every night.

## Script 3 premise: The morning laugh

Rule: in this town, the sun only rises if someone laughs before 6 a.m. Gags: a professional laugher on the clock tower, alarm clocks that tell jokes, the grumpiest man in town lives in permanent night. Exception: a kid who can't laugh. Twist: the professional laugher quit years ago; the kid's little sister has been sneaking up the tower every morning. Payoff: the sister gets sick, the sun doesn't rise, and the grumpy man's first laugh in 40 years, at the kid's terrible joke, brings it up.
